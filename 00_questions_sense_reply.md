# Questions for Sense Reply

> Rule: ask for **numbers and examples**, not opinions. Avoid yes/no questions.
> If time is short, ask only the **Top 10** first.

## Top 10 (must ask)

1. **Today, who pays you for this platform or its predecessors (RIPA, smart grid, demand response)?** Which segments bring most of the revenue: DSOs, aggregators, industrial clients, others?
2. **How many edge gateways does a typical customer deploy?** Give us a range for a DSO, an aggregator and an industrial site.
3. Of your existing clients (Italy, Netherlands, TotalEnergies, Iceland…), **which would be the first to buy the new platform, and why them?**
4. **How long is a typical sales cycle**, from first meeting to signed contract, for a DSO compared with a private company?
5. **Who signs the contract** at the client (CEO, CTO, head of operations, procurement)? And who usually blocks the deal?
6. When you lose a deal, **who do you lose it to?** Is it a competitor, an internal IT team building its own solution, or the client simply doing nothing?
7. **What do clients pay today for a comparable project?** We need an order of magnitude for integration fees and for yearly run costs.
8. **Product or service:** what share of a typical project is integration and custom work, and what share is reusable platform?
9. Can the agents **act directly** on assets, or do they only **propose** actions for a human to validate? How do you see the **AI Act** affecting grid use cases?
10. **Where do you want to be in 3 years?** For example: X clients, Y gateways, a target share of recurring revenue.

## Product & roadmap

- What is **already in production** with real clients, and what is still prototype or demo?
- Which **gateway hardware** is supported? Can the platform run on third-party gateways, such as those from battery or inverter OEMs?
- How long does it take to **onboard a new site** today, in days or weeks? What is the main bottleneck?
- Is the platform **tied to AWS**, or can it run on Azure or on-premise? Do some clients refuse public cloud?
- For on-device LLMs (Mistral): what is the **concrete benefit compared with classical optimisation** (rules, linear programming)? Is there a measured result?
- What are the **next 3 features** on the roadmap?

## Customers & segmentation

- Which segments do you **not** want to target, and why?
- Are **energy communities** paying customers, or a use case you sell to utilities and DSOs?
- Which **countries** are a priority after Italy? Are there markets where Reply already has strong energy clients?
- What does a **"bad" customer** look like for you, one that cost a lot and brought little?
- Do you have a **measured ROI** from an existing client, such as savings in €, kWh or avoided grid investment?

## Sales & go-to-market

- How do you sell today? Through a dedicated sales team, Reply account managers, or partners such as AWS or system integrators?
- How do you handle the **IT vs. business gap** inside a client? Who do you convince first?
- What does a typical **pilot** look like (duration, price, success criteria)? How many pilots turn into contracts?
- Do public **tenders** play a big role with DSOs? Are there tender frameworks you are already listed on?

## Competition

- Which names come up most often during deals? We are checking AWS IoT Greengrass, Azure IoT, Siemens, Schneider, Barbara IoT, gridX, Litmus.
- Is **AWS a partner or a competitor** for this platform?
- In one sentence, why should a client choose you over **building in-house** on AWS?

## Business model & pricing

- Do you prefer **recurring revenue** (licence or subscription) or **project revenue**? What does Reply's group management expect?
- Is **outcome-based pricing** (paid on savings) realistic for you? Have you ever done it?
- What **pricing unit** makes sense: per gateway, per asset, per MW, per site, or per user?
- For DSOs: do they prefer **capex or opex** because of how they are regulated?

## Partners & channels

- Which partners already bring you deals: AWS, hardware makers, battery integrators, ESCOs?
- Would you accept a **white-label / OEM** model, where a battery maker ships your platform inside its product?

## Link with the B2 team (pilot data)

- Which **KPIs** would convince a client to buy, such as € saved, CO₂ avoided, or battery cycles?
- Can we use the B2 results as a **business case** in our presentation?
